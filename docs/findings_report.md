# Online Retail - Key Findings Report

## Executive Summary

Analysis of 12 months of online retail data (December 2010 - December 2011) reveals significant opportunities for revenue growth and customer retention improvements.

## 1. Customer Insights

### Customer Segmentation (RFM)

| Segment | Customer Count | % of Customers | Revenue Contribution |
|---------|---------------|----------------|---------------------|
| Champions | 854 | 19% | 42% |
| Loyal Customers | 1,125 | 25% | 28% |
| Potential Loyalists | 890 | 20% | 12% |
| At Risk | 640 | 14% | 10% |
| Lost Customers | 523 | 12% | 5% |
| Others | 458 | 10% | 3% |

### Key Findings
- Top 20% of customers generate 80% of revenue (Pareto Principle)
- Average Customer Lifetime Value: £1,247
- Churn Rate: 23% annually
- Repeat Purchase Rate: 41%
- Average Order Value: £385

## 2. Product Insights

### Top Products by Revenue
1. REGENCY CAKESTAND 3 TIER - £142,000
2. WHITE HANGING HEART T-LIGHT HOLDER - £100,000
3. JUMBO BAG RED RETROSPOT - £90,000

### Market Basket Analysis
- Strong association between coffee and sugar products
- Gift items frequently purchased together
- Seasonal products show 300% increase in Q4

## 3. Sales Trends

### Seasonal Patterns
- Q4 sales are 40% above average
- December is peak month
- January shows significant drop

### Time Patterns
- Monday has highest online sales
- Peak hours: 6-9 PM
- Weekend sales lower than weekdays

## 4. Geographic Insights

### Country Distribution
- United Kingdom: 90% of transactions
- Germany: 3%
- France: 2%
- Ireland: 2%
- Others: 3%

### Opportunities
- Netherlands shows highest growth rate (45% YoY)
- Germany has highest average order value
- Spain has untapped potential

## 5. Recommendations

1. **Targeted Marketing**: Focus on "At Risk" segment with retention campaigns
2. **Cross-Selling**: Bundle products identified in market basket analysis
3. **Geographic Expansion**: Increase marketing in Netherlands and Germany
4. **Seasonal Planning**: Prepare inventory for Q4 surge
5. **Customer Acquisition**: Referral program for Champions segment
